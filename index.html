Privacy Policy — Plovdiv Transport

Last updated: October 2026

Plovdiv Transport ("the app") is a free Android application developed by Ivan Chaushev. This page explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

Information we collect

The app requests access to your device's location (GPS) solely to identify nearby bus stops. Location data is used only within the app on your device and is never transmitted to our servers, stored persistently, or shared with any third party.

Location access is optional. You can use route planning and the map without granting location permission.

Third-party services

The app fetches real-time bus departure data from livetransport.eu, the official feed for Plovdiv public transport. Your requests to that service include only the stop ID you are querying — no personal information.

Map tiles are provided by MapTiler / OpenStreetMap contributors. Standard tile requests contain only the map coordinates being viewed.

Data storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your recent stop and route searches (for convenience — never leaves your device)
  • A cached copy of bus stop data (to allow offline use)
  • Your chosen app language (Bulgarian or English)

None of this data is shared with us or any third party.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
